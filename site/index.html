The handoff

Out of usage? Not your problem.

Add your accounts once. When one hits its limit, Cmdhive moves the same conversation to the next and carries on. When every account of one AI is out, another AI takes over with the whole conversation.

Features

    
Built for people who work with AI all day.

    

      

        

        
Every AI, one window

        
Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI in a real chat view: streaming replies, rendered markdown, code you can copy, a model picker for each.

      

      

        

        
Never runs out

        
Several accounts, one conversation. Cmdhive switches to the next account by itself and tells you when the first one is back.

      

      

        

        
One shared memory

        
What one AI learns, they all know. Your preferences and project decisions live in one memory every AI reads and adds to.

      

      

        

        
All your devices

        
Your desktop and your laptop share that memory through a folder you already sync, like OneDrive or iCloud. No server, no account.

      

      

        

        
A browser it can drive

        
Watch the AI browse and playtest like a live stream, its steps beside the page. When a site needs a person, like a CAPTCHA, it hands over to you.

      

      

        

        
You stay in control

        
Every edit and command can wait for you on an approval card: allow it once, always, or deny it. Or switch the questions off.

      

    

    

      
      
    

  






  

    
Privacy

    
No account. No server. No telemetry.

    
Cmdhive runs the AI tools you already use, on your own computer. Nothing about you goes anywhere it doesn't have to.

    

      
Your chats stay yoursThey stay where the AI tools already keep them. Cmdhive has nothing of its own to send them to.

      
Logins are never readCmdhive only checks that a login exists, to tell whether you're signed in. It never opens one.

      
Verified updatesUpdates come only from the open-source releases on GitHub, and install only if they match their published SHA-256.
